About the Parliament of Ghana

The Parliament of Ghana is the legislative branch of the Government of Ghana. It is a unicameral legislature consisting of 275 members who are elected to serve four-year terms. The Parliament is responsible for enacting laws, approving the national budget, and providing oversight of the executive branch.

The Parliament of Ghana was first established as a legislative body during the independence of Ghana in 1957. Since then, it has evolved through various constitutional amendments and political changes, playing a crucial role in Ghana's democratic development.

Did You Know?

The Parliament of Ghana is housed in a modern complex located in Osu, Accra, featuring state-of-the-art facilities for legislative debates, committee meetings, and public engagement.
